The Journey

From pavement
to promise

Lavos wasn't born in a boardroom — it was born on a street corner, with a folding table, a thermos, and a stubborn belief that coffee could be more than just a morning ritual.

My friend started with a cart, a smile, and the freshest beans he could find. No big budget, no fancy shop — just pure, unfiltered hustle. Muffins still warm from the oven, scones that crumbled just right, and coffee that made strangers stop and stay.

Every day was a lesson. Every cup was a conversation. And slowly, the street became a community. That's the Lavos way — building something real, one pour at a time.

Day 1 · The Cart

A folding table, a thermos, and a dream. First cup served at 6:17 AM.

Week 6 · The Regulars

Faces became familiar. Names turned into stories. The cart felt like home.

Month 4 · The Fire

Late nights, early mornings. Burned batches and broken mugs — but never the spirit.

Now · The Vision

From street corner to brand. Lavos is a story of grit, grace, and great coffee.
